Plant Care Guide

Our pots are designed to support healthy plant growth. Here are some tips to get the most out of your PotHub planter:

  • Ensure drainage holes are clear before planting.
  • Use a saucer to catch excess water and prevent surface damage.
  • For ceramic pots, avoid thermal shock by not moving from cold to hot environments suddenly.
  • Clean with a damp cloth. Avoid harsh chemical cleaners.
  • For terracotta pots, soak in water for 30 minutes before first use.
  • Store outdoor pots indoors during extreme weather to extend lifespan.
