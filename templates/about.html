How We Grew

OUR JOURNEY

2019
The First Pot

PotHub started in a small Manila studio apartment. Founder Anna Reyes hand-threw her first terracotta pot and posted it online. It sold in 4 minutes.

2020
Going Online

During the pandemic, indoor gardening exploded. PotHub launched its online store and hit ₱1M in sales in the first 3 months.

2021
The Studio Opens

We opened our first physical studio in Makati, featuring a ceramics workshop, plant market, and the full PotHub catalog.

2023
10,000 Plant Parents

We crossed 10,000 happy customers and expanded our catalog to 500+ unique pot styles. National shipping launched.

2026
The Future Is Green

We launched our Eco Series, introduced biodegradable packaging, and set a goal to plant 10,000 trees by 2027.
